Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download, access, and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (for example, IP address)
  • The pages or screens of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages or screens

  • The operating system and device type you use on your mobile device
  • Account information you provide when signing in, such as your name, email address, and profile details
  • Content you create, upload, record, transcribe, generate, export, or store in the Application, including audio files, transcripts, notes, study materials, calendar-generated content, presentations, and related metadata

The Service Provider may use the information you provide and the information collected automatically to operate the Application, authenticate your account, provide support, improve features, maintain security, process subscriptions, communicate important notices, and, where permitted by law, send service-related updates and promotional communications.

Google User Data

When you sign in with Google or use Google-integrated features, the Application may access the following Google user data:

  • Google Account Profile Information: Your name, email address, and basic profile information, used to create and manage your Scribe-E account.
  • Google Calendar: Read and write access to calendar events, used to create calendar events from your transcriptions, recordings, notes, and action items. The Application may create events on your primary Google Calendar and attach documents to those events.
  • Google Drive: File creation access, used to create Google Docs containing transcription content or related exported content.
  • Google Slides: Presentation creation and editing access, used to create Google Slides presentations and slide decks generated from your content.

How We Use Google User Data

Google user data is used solely to provide the Application's core features and user-requested functionality. Specifically:

  • Your Google profile information, such as your name and email address, may be stored in Firebase Firestore to identify your account, manage your subscription, and associate your content with your profile.
  • Google Calendar access is used only when you enable or use calendar-related functionality, including creating events from recordings, transcriptions, notes, or action items.
  • Google Drive and Google Slides access is used only when you explicitly create or export documents or slide decks, or when the Application generates supporting Google documents for features you initiate.
  • OAuth authentication tokens may be stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms to maintain your signed-in session and enable authorized Google-integrated features.

How We Store Google User Data

Your Google profile information, such as name and email address, may be stored securely in Firebase services operated by Google, including Firebase Authentication and Firebase Firestore. OAuth tokens used to maintain your signed-in session are stored locally on your device using platform-appropriate secure storage. Content you choose to export to Google Docs or Google Slides is created within your own Google account. The Service Provider does not use Google user data for advertising purposes.
